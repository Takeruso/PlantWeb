About Plant Category

  1. Family

    Family is a higher-level category in plant classification that includes multiple genera (plural of genus) with similar characteristics.

  2. Genus

    Genus is a more specific level of classification below the family, representing a group of plants with similar characteristics

  3. Species

    Species is the most specific level of plant classification, where plants of the same species are genetically similar and can interbreed to produce offspring
